About

The APA New York Metro Chapter Technology Committee — connecting planners with the tools that shape cities.

Our Mission

The APA NYM Technology Committee serves the New York Metro chapter — one of APA's largest regional chapters, covering New York City, Long Island, and the lower Hudson Valley — by curating technology-focused educational content, advising on digital tools for planning practice, connecting members with technology resources, and supporting the chapter's digital presence and communication.

What We Do

  • Educational programming — Technology-focused sessions at NYM events, including Tech Tables and conference workshops
  • Tool guidance — Recommendations for planning software, platforms, and data resources
  • Member resources — Tutorials, templates, best practices, and the BYTE BY BYTE quarterly newsletter
  • Industry connections — Vendor relationships and technology partnerships for the planning community

Get Involved

We're always looking for planners, technologists, designers, and researchers who want to shape how technology serves planning practice. Whether you're a seasoned professional or just getting started, there's a place for you.

Join our listserv to stay in the loop, or reach out to our co-chairs at technology@nyplanning.org.
