# Committee governance digest

## Status and sources

- **Technology Committee bylaws:** [official working draft](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1pR0bVOv1AB9M-Y_hDeLm78YINieiC4eOyknFUCc7pOY/edit). The document labels itself “Draft” and says it was adapted from the APA Technology Division bylaws adopted in November 2025. This project must not describe the Committee draft as adopted unless the APA New York Metro Chapter confirms adoption.
- **APA New York Metro Chapter bylaws:** [official chapter PDF](https://www.nyplanning.org/s/bylaws.pdf), titled “Bylaws New York Metropolitan Chapter of the American Planning Association as Amended September 2013.” These are the parent Chapter bylaws to which the Committee draft is subordinate.

## Governance summary

The draft defines the Technology Committee as a committee of the APA New York Metro Chapter and makes it subject to the Chapter bylaws and Chapter Board approval. Its purpose is to build a regional network of planners, share resources on data and technology, educate the Chapter on policy and practice, coordinate programs and research, and promote inclusive, accountable, and data-informed uses of technology in planning.

The draft calls for elected Chair, Vice-Chair, Secretary, and Treasurer positions and permits appointed supporting roles such as a Social Media Coordinator or GitHub Administrator. It assigns operational decisions to the officers by majority vote, requires Chapter Board approval for significant policy positions or public statements, and provides that non-Chair vacancies be filled by an officer vote or special election as appropriate. Officer elections would occur every two years in alignment with the Chapter election cycle, but these procedures remain proposals while the document is a draft.

The adopted Chapter bylaws authorize Chapter committees and place overall policy, financial, and administrative authority with the Chapter Executive Committee. They also establish Chapter-level Secretary and Treasurer duties, financial controls, committee oversight, and approval requirements that provide the parent framework for the Committee draft.

## Website role copy

The Committee page uses the site’s existing **Co-Chair** terminology rather than presenting the draft’s Chair/Vice-Chair structure as adopted. Its four open-role cards summarize these draft responsibilities:

- **Co-Chair:** meeting and activity leadership, special projects and events, collaboration, and leadership continuity, drawn from draft Sections 4.2–4.3.
- **Secretary:** minutes, Chapter reporting, participant communications, records, and archives, drawn from draft Section 4.4.
- **Treasurer:** budget support, compliant fund management, financial reporting, and accurate contribution records, drawn from draft Section 4.5 and Article VIII.
- **Communications:** regular participant updates, current web/publication content, and APA/APA-NYM brand alignment, drawn from draft Article IX and the draft’s allowance for appointed communications roles in Section 4.1.

The vacancy cards are code-defined governance content rather than `members` collection records. This avoids creating fictional people or treating unfilled positions as roster members; Andrew Buck remains the only seeded member in `scripts/seed.ts`.
