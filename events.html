Spring Tech Table

June 18, 2026 6-8pm VHB - One Penn Plaza, New York

We are excited to introduce Tech Tables as a new format for Tech Committee meetings. This is our quarterly meeting that brings together planners, technologists, and innovators for meaningful conversations about the intersection of technology and the planning and placemaking. When attending a Tech Table, please be ready to participate in an open discussion about current technology trends impacting planning and design, member projects that are being shared for community feedback and advice, and special lectures and events. // 2026 SPRING TECH TABLE Date: Thursday, June 18, 2026 Time: 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM EDT Venue: VHB Office, One Penn Plaza, NYC Hybrid option offered to members who RSVP CM Credits to be Provided for Planning with a Black Mirror Session (Credits TBD) Light Snacks and Refreshments to be provided Kickoff of Tech Tables, conducting Tech Committee reflecting on the year so far, what is coming up in 2026, and open discussion on planning ahead. Wrap-up includes a one-hour happy hour to celebrate the release of the Planning with a Black Mirror card game. The American Planning Association's (APA) Technology Division created a scenario-based card game, to help planners collaboratively explore the impacts of emerging technologies and unpredictable events on communities. Please come expecting to learn and play with your fellow planners!
